## What this proves and what it does not

Proves: both criterion suites compile in release mode and execute to completion
at this commit. Does not prove: any design target, any sustained-throughput or
resource figure, or a `pass` on the `Benchmarks` gate row. Issue #157 remains
open and blocked until the full procedure in
`docs/benchmarks/verification-package.md` is run on the stabilization commit
and reference hardware.
